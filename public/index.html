🌑 Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Welcome, cadets! You are 3 junior astronauts training at the Moon's south pole. Your Dungeon Master is , who controls everything that happens.

  • 🎲 Roll: on your turn, say what you want to do and roll a d20. The DM decides what you're rolling for and what happens. 1 backfires, 2-3 are really bad, 10 usually slides, 18-20 are amazing.
  • 💬 Talk: talk to the DM or the astronauts at moon bases any time. Talking doesn't use your turn.
  • 🎒 Items: you can only use what's in your inventory. Want more? Search things like rocks and crates.
  • ❤️ HP: lose it all and you get knocked out until the next location. It's a simulation, so you can't really lose.
  • ⭐ Points: totally meaningless. The DM gives or takes them based on vibes.
  • 🗺️ Goal: clear 5 real lunar south pole locations and defeat the Giant Moon Worm.

Pass the device around, or play all three yourself.
